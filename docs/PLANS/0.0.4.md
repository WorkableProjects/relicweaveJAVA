## 0. Non-negotiable implementation rules

1. **Audit before editing.** Inspect the current source, registry IDs, data files, recipes, saved-data handling, configs, and existing tests before implementing any feature. Do not infer the exact behavior of an item from its display name.
2. **Preserve 0.0.2.** Existing items, enchantments, infusions, machine behavior, recipes, registry IDs, and world/player data must continue working. In particular, Iron Ingots and Nether Stars already have an enchantment mapping and an infusion mapping in the existing design; do not replace, repurpose, or overwrite either mapping.
3. **Preserve the planned 0.0.3 mapping matrix exactly.** Treat it as a protected compatibility contract (Section 8). Each listed source item must retain both its Transmutation Table enchantment recipe and its Celestial Infuser infusion recipe. Never make an item map to only one of those pathways.
4. **Do not collide with established content.** New 0.0.4 names, item IDs, recipe IDs, loot tables, structures, tags, sounds, and effects must be checked for duplicates against 0.0.2 and planned 0.0.3. Rename a proposed addition if necessary; never silently change an older mapping to make room.
5. **Keep third-party mods optional.** RelicWeave must run without Curios or any animation/content library. Use guarded integrations and matching Forge 1.20.1 dependency versions where supported.
6. **Server authority.** Dimension progression, boss health/phases, recipe consumption, loot, cooldowns, hazards, and relic effects must be validated server-side. Client code may render feedback but must not grant items or effects.
7. **Build in vertical slices.** Implement and test a small complete slice of world generation, one biome, one structure, one mob, one hazard, and one loot path before expanding the catalog.
8. **Do not release half-working content.** The flagship scope is broad, but every planned feature needs a working implementation or an explicit, documented decision to defer it before the release is tagged.

## 1. Vision and player journey

**Working dimension name:** **The Vestige**  
**Suggested dimension ID:** `relicweave:the_vestige`  
**Civilization:** The Concordance, an ancient society that used relics and monumental machines to stabilize reality. Their empire collapsed when they tried to synchronize every region of their world into one permanent magical cycle. The surviving ruins, machines, and creatures are fragments of that failed experiment.

The Vestige must feel as consequential as a major vanilla dimension—not like a recolored Nether or End. Its identity comes from colossal ruined architecture, vertical fractured landscapes, crystal-root forests, sideways-running water, ancient civic machinery, unstable local gravity, and a recurring world cycle that changes exploration and combat.

### Intended journey

1. **Unlock:** Defeat the Ender Dragon. The player discovers how to assemble and activate a Concordance Gate using End-era ingredients, without needing materials that can only be obtained inside The Vestige.
2. **Arrive:** Spawn at a deliberately safe arrival region with a visible ancient waystone, a readable tutorial inscription, and a route toward the first ruins. The player has time to learn the dimension before encountering the harshest hazards.
3. **Explore:** Discover biomes, ruins, inscriptions, rare caches, and resource deposits. Learn how the world cycle changes threats and opportunities.
4. **Prepare:** Craft hazard protection, obtain the first relics, and gather common dimension materials. Deeper regions expect strong enchanted Netherite gear or equivalent preparation.
5. **Progress:** Defeat regional bosses to earn unique cores and unlock crafting templates, relic augmentations, and the Aetherforged equipment tier.
6. **Master:** Combine dimension relics, new enchantments, existing Celestial Infusions, and environmental knowledge to reach the Crown of Concordance.
7. **Finish:** Defeat Archivist-Prime, receive the defining endgame rewards, and unlock repeatable high-risk exploration rather than ending the dimension after a single boss kill.

### Experience pillars

- **Discovery:** Major structures should be readable from the landscape and contain clues, secrets, or loot worth reaching.
- **Preparation:** Hazards telegraph danger and offer counterplay through gear, consumables, relics, and route choice.
- **Combat mastery:** Boss attacks are clearly telegraphed and use distinct mechanics, not just oversized health bars.
- **Progression:** Every major resource has a clear use in gear, relics, infusions, enchantments, structures, or crafting.
- **Replayability:** World cycles, multiple regional routes, loot variations, and rematchable bosses support revisits.

## 2. Dimension entry and world-generation foundation

### Concordance Gate

- Introduce a craftable **Gate Keystone** and **Concordance Frame** blocks. These are new RelicWeave IDs; they must not replace or modify an existing portal, machine, or recipe.
- Activation requires the Ender Dragon advancement (`minecraft:end/kill_dragon`) and a crafted **Threshold Sigil** made only from ingredients available before entering The Vestige. Candidate ingredients: Dragon's Breath, Amethyst Shards, Chorus Fruit, and End Stone Bricks. Do not require Aetherite, boss drops, Iron Ingots, Nether Stars, or any 0.0.3 mapping catalyst to enter.
- The frame should be visually and mechanically distinct from a Nether portal. It may use a ritual interaction and a short, interruptible activation sequence.
- Validate the frame and advancement on the server. Prevent duplicate activation, unsafe teleport destinations, and portal loops.
- Return travel must be reliable. Store a safe return position/dimension when useful; handle death, unloaded chunks, removed frames, and server restart safely.
- Include a `/execute in relicweave:the_vestige` debug route for development only; normal progression must not rely on commands.

### World-generation implementation

- Use the project's current Forge 1.20.1 worldgen and registry approach; verify exact APIs against Forge 47.3.0+ and the existing MDK before coding.
- Prefer data-driven biome, placed-feature, configured-feature, structure, loot-table, dimension-type, and noise-settings resources when possible. Use custom Java code only when the desired behavior cannot be expressed safely through existing data systems.
- Register the dimension without replacing vanilla dimensions or changing existing world presets.
- Establish a consistent dimension height range, spawn safety rules, fog/sky rendering, ambient lighting, music boundaries, and map/compass behavior.
- Use staged generation: first prove dimension registration, safe arrival, one representative biome, and one structure; then expand the biome catalog.
- Avoid expensive custom chunk-generation loops and huge numbers of block entities. Structure pieces and feature placement must respect chunk boundaries, world height, and server generation limits.

## 4. World cycles and environmental hazards

### Concordance Cycle

Use a server-side dimension cycle with three named phases. Exact durations are configuration values and must be play-tested; suggested initial total cycle: about 18–24 in-game minutes.

1. **Quiet:** Normal baseline spawns and safest exploration window. Some caches are dormant; hazard accumulation is slow.
2. **Resonance:** Ruin runes light up, certain resources become easier to identify, elite patrols become more active, and some sealed pathways open.
3. **Convergence:** Highest-risk phase. Rift activity and boss-region hazards intensify, rare event enemies can appear, and special resource nodes may activate. A clear audio/visual warning must precede the phase.

Rules:

- Phase changes must be deterministic, synchronized, and driven by server world time or saved dimension data; do not update the entire dimension every tick.
- Show the current phase through a HUD/tooltip entry, Cycle Dial relic, ancient waystones, and relevant guidebook pages.
- Use configurable phase durations and hazard strengths. Avoid changing the global Overworld/Nether/End clock.
- A server restart must not reset the cycle in a way that grants repeated loot or free hazard immunity.
- Multiplayer players in the same dimension should agree on the current phase. Handle late joins and players entering/leaving safely.

### Hazard set

| Hazard | Where it appears | Behavior and counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| **Rift Saturation** | Marked fissures and deeper ruins. | Exposure accumulates while unprotected, then applies a brief, bounded debuff. Use clear visual/audio warning stages; exposure decays outside the zone or with valid protection. Never cause unavoidable death from an entry spawn. |
| **Gravity Shear** | Sundered Reach, selected vault chambers. | Short pulls, altered jump control, or predictable displacement pulses. Telegraph direction and safe timing; do not continuously override the player's controls. |
| **Starfall Impact** | Starfall Caldera during event windows. | Mark an impact area, play a warning cue, then strike it after a delay. Players can dodge or use cover. No invisible instant damage. |
| **Null Pockets** | Rare sealed rooms and boss arenas. | Temporarily suppress specific relic abilities or reduce their effectiveness; clearly communicate which effects are suppressed. Never silently delete cooldowns or item charges. |
| **Hushfield** | Hushfen and parts of Gloam Archive. | Fog/ambient-audio change and sound-sensitive enemy behavior. Do not make important warning audio inaccessible; pair essential warnings with visual indicators/subtitles. |

### Hazard protections

- Introduce craftable, consumable **Ward Salves** and/or wearable protection based on new dimension materials.
- The new relics and infusion effects in Sections 6–8 may mitigate hazards, but no single item should make every hazard irrelevant.
- Display exposure status through an unobtrusive meter/icon, not a full-screen overlay that permanently obscures combat.
- Keep all damage/debuff values configurable and bounded. Environmental effects must respect server difficulty settings where appropriate.
- Avoid affecting players outside The Vestige, and ensure the hazard manager does not run expensive checks for unloaded chunks.

## 6. Six major bosses

The flagship target is **six major bosses**, including the final boss. Each regional boss controls a meaningful region/structure and unlocks a new progression step. Every fight needs readable attack telegraphs, a fair arena, server-authoritative phases, anti-softlock recovery, and a unique reward.

| Boss | Region / arena | Encounter design | Core reward / progression |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The Thorned Regent** | Glassroot Wilds · Root-Crown Court | Controls roots that section off the arena, summons limited stalkers, and exposes its core after a heavy root strike. | **Sovereign Thorn**; first major relic upgrade component and one Aetherforged material ingredient. |
| **2. The Choir Unheard** | Hushfen · Drowned Conservatory | Layered sound/rune patterns, marked silence zones, and a sequence where players must move between safe tones. Essential cues also use visuals/subtitles. | **Liminal Chord**; unlocks a deeper Archive path and sound/resonance crafting. |
| **3. The Gravity Maw** | Sundered Reach · Inverted Vault | Predictable pull/push phases, falling debris with marked impact zones, and safe anchors that can be used tactically. Avoid actual camera inversion or controls that make the fight inaccessible. | **Gravity Heart**; key upgrade catalyst for traversal gear and relics. |
| **4. The Starforged Colossus** | Starfall Caldera · Foundry of Falling Stars | Slow, destructive strikes and heat pulses; players use cover and activate vents/forges during brief vulnerability windows. | **Starforged Core**; unlocks advanced alloy processing. |
| **5. The Warden of Seals** | Gloam Archive · Hall of Nine Seals | Rotating protective runes and summoned guardians; the player must disable exposed seal nodes between attack cycles. | **Concordant Seal**; unlocks final-region gates and high-tier smithing template access. |
| **6. Archivist-Prime** | Crown of Concordance · The Grand Index | Multi-stage final encounter that combines cycle shifts, seal mechanics, and arena positioning. Each stage introduces at most one new rule; no opaque one-shot attacks. | **Prime Mnemonic Core** and final recipe/loot pool; unlocks repeatable endgame trials or rematches. |

### Boss rules

- Bosses are not simply larger mobs with inflated health. Each must have at least three readable moves or phases, clear recovery windows, a distinct arena gimmick, and a tested solo route.
- Add configuration for boss health/damage multipliers, arena hazard strength, and whether boss respawns/rematches are enabled. The default balance must remain fair without relying on a config adjustment.
- Boss completion and rewards must be idempotent: relogging, server restart, or duplicated event delivery must not grant duplicate one-time unlocks.
- If a player dies or disconnects, the boss encounter must recover without permanently locking the structure. Define retreat/reset behavior explicitly.
- Support multiplayer without relying on one player being the permanent target. Scale pressure modestly by player count without linear health inflation becoming excessive.
- Ensure boss loot is granted by server-side loot tables/validated encounter state, not by client animation events.

### 8.2 Aetherforged gear tier

Add a new **Aetherforged** tier as a post-Netherite endgame sidegrade with stronger durability and specialized utility, not unlimited power creep.

- Implement armor, sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and any additional tool only after the base tier passes balance testing.
- Prefer a smithing-template upgrade path that preserves enchantments, custom names, and normal item metadata. Obtain the **Vestige Upgrade Template** from a guaranteed mid/late-dimension progression reward; allow duplication only through a clear and costly recipe.
- Use Concordant Alloy and selected boss components for upgrades. Do not require a Nether Star or Iron Ingot as the unique key ingredient, and do not alter any existing recipe using them.
- Establish explicit comparison tests against Netherite: durability, armor/toughness, attack damage/speed, mining speed, enchantability, repair behavior, and knockback resistance. Use conservative starting values; target modest durability gains and at most small stat improvements, while allowing a distinct utility perk where balanced.
- Avoid a powerful full-set bonus that renders Celestial Infusions or relics obsolete. Equipment tier, infusions, and relics should complement rather than replace each other.
- Ensure items can be repaired, enchanted, renamed, upgraded, and stored normally. Validate creative mode, loot, recipe output, anvil behavior, and existing item migration.

### 8.3 Reliquary Loom — relic upgrades and customization

Introduce a new **Reliquary Loom** workstation for optional relic customization. It must not replace or change the Transmutation Table or Celestial Infuser.

- Start by supporting the new 0.0.4 relics and a curated list of existing relics only after compatibility is explicitly tested. Do not silently rebalance every 0.0.2/0.0.3 relic.
- Each supported relic may select **one of two mutually exclusive augment paths** (for example, Exploration vs. Stability), with clear effect text and costs. Choices should create sidegrades instead of purely linear stat inflation.
- Use Mnemonic Filament, boss components, and other new dimension materials. No random reroll loop, paid re-roll requirement, or irreversible hidden choice.
- Show preview, cost, valid relics, and whether the selected augment is permanent before confirming. Validate all crafting and item state on the server.
- Preserve the relic's base identity, registry ID, cooldown state, custom name, and unrelated NBT/data. Augments must not reset cooldowns or duplicate effects when moving through inventory, hands, or Curios slots.
- Add a safe migration format/version for augmented relic data. Unsupported/invalid augment data must fail safely and preserve the base relic.

| **Threshold Sigil** | Dragon's Breath, Amethyst Shards, Chorus Fruit, End Stone Bricks | Craftable before dimension entry. Does not use dimension-only drops, Iron Ingots, Nether Stars, or the protected 0.0.3 mapping catalysts. |
| **Concordance Frame blocks** | Purpur Blocks, End Stone Bricks, Amethyst Shards, and a common vanilla binding ingredient | Craft multiple frame blocks per recipe; require a valid frame shape and Threshold Sigil to activate. Do not replace vanilla portal blocks. |
| **Ward Salve** | Common Aetherite-derived ingredient plus Riftglass and a vanilla container/consumable component | Made after entry; provides temporary, partial Rift Saturation protection. No infinite protection or instant full heal. |
| **Aetherite processing** | Aetherite Shards smelted or processed into Aetherite Ingots/Dust | Provide a straightforward common-resource route so basic crafting is not gated behind a rare boss drop. |
| **Riftglass Lens** | Riftglass plus Amethyst Shards or another verified common optic component | Must be craftable from a predictable recipe or appear as a guaranteed cache reward; it is required for the 0.0.4 mapping route. |
| **Vestige Upgrade Template** | Guaranteed Warden of Seals reward or a guaranteed route in the Hall of Nine Seals | Must be obtainable even if a random chest roll fails. Template duplication is costly but possible; avoid a one-time irreversible drop that can be lost. |
| **Aetherforged equipment** | Existing Netherite equipment + Vestige Upgrade Template + Concordant Alloy in the smithing-table path | Preserve enchantments, names, and item metadata. Recipes must not overwrite the vanilla Netherite upgrade recipe. |
| **Concordant Alloy** | Aetherite Ingots plus one or more mid/late dimension components; boss components may improve or unlock batches | Do not require a single unique boss drop per armor/tool piece. Rematch or crafting routes must supply enough material for a full progression path. |
| **Reliquary Loom** | Vestige Stone, Aetherite Ingots, and Mnemonic Filament | New workstation only; does not replace or modify the Transmutation Table or Celestial Infuser. |
| **0.0.4 enchantments / infusions** | Section 8.5 catalysts through separate machine recipe IDs | Every source item keeps both paths. Recipe inputs/costs must be validated against actual 0.0.2 and planned 0.0.3 recipes before registration. |
| **0.0.4 relics** | Mix of structure rewards, crafted components, and boss rewards | A relic may be guaranteed via a progression path or crafted after discovery. Do not make every relic depend on an ultra-low random drop. |

**Progression-softlock protection:** The Thorned Regent must drop enough Sovereign Thorns for both its intended progression unlock and at least one crafting route, with a rematch or conversion route for additional supply. The Warden of Seals must grant a Concordant Seal for progression and provide additional seals or a predictable repeatable acquisition route so players do not have to choose between opening content and crafting an infusion/enchantment. Keep the final-gate key separate from consumable catalyst counts when feasible.

### 8.7 Four infusion rules

- Keep the existing Celestial Infuser's current infusion-count limit and application/removal semantics. Do not increase the limit in 0.0.4 without a separate balance decision.
- Verify that Riftward, Sealguard, Cycleweave, and Gravitic Tread do not duplicate any of the existing nine 0.0.2 infusions or the four planned 0.0.3 infusions (Tidal, Anchored, Warding, Pursuit). Rename or redesign conflicts after the registry audit.
- Define repeated-infusion stacking explicitly. Default: identical infusion effects do not stack; multiple eligible sources share one effect instance or use the strongest single value.
- Make armor swaps, inventory changes, death, respawn, dimension transfer, and reconnect safe. Client HUDs must reflect server state without granting effects.

## 9. Six new relics

Do not duplicate the planned 0.0.3 relic set (Lodestone Charm, Tidal Charm, Featherwoven Charm, Wayfarer's Relic, Echo Charm, Guardian Relic). The following 0.0.4 relics are proposed around exploration, cycle mastery, and hazard counterplay.

| Relic | Effect | Balance / implementation limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Surveyor's Lens** | Active pulse reveals hidden inscriptions, false walls, and nearby structure clues. | Limited radius, clear outline duration, cooldown; does not reveal container contents or boss health through walls. |
| **Gravity Nail** | Temporarily anchors the wearer against a predictable gravity pulse. | Short duration and cooldown; does not permit flight or nullify every knockback source. |
| **Cycle Dial** | Displays the current Concordance Cycle and gives a warning before the next transition. | Information utility, not a cycle skip; no duplicate HUD widgets when equipped in several locations. |
| **Riftglass Lantern** | Creates a small temporary safe radius that slows Rift Saturation accumulation. | Limited radius/charge or cooldown; cannot protect an entire structure or bypass boss mechanics. |
| **Mnemonic Spindle** | Interacts with eligible ancient terminals to decode one clue or reveal a route branch. | Must not unlock boss doors or grant guaranteed rare loot without completing the puzzle. |
| **Sealkeeper's Signet** | Briefly disrupts non-boss Vestige wardens or opens a designated low-tier seal during exploration. | No boss stun-lock; server checks eligible targets and structures; clear cooldown. |

### Relic behavior

- Use the existing shared relic activation/selection approach from the 0.0.3 plan; do not add six unrelated keybinds.
- Support Curios only through the existing optional integration layer. Without Curios, relics must work through the currently supported inventory/hand behavior.
- Prevent duplicate effects from copies in inventory, offhand, armor, and Curios. Cooldowns persist through item movement, death, respawn, disconnect, and dimension changes.
- Respect server configuration and multiplayer rules. Do not store critical cooldowns only on the client or solely in a transient object.

## 10. Visual direction, sound, and presentation

### Visual identity

- **Palette:** aged ivory stone, weathered gold, oxidized teal, ink-blue shadows, translucent opal/crystal, with restrained violet accents for unstable reality.
- **Silhouette:** enormous civic arches, broken vertical bridges, suspended aqueducts, tower stacks, ring-shaped observatories, roots intertwined with architecture, and a distant central city.
- **Block family:** Vestige Stone variants, cracked/carved blocks, gilded inlays, rune-bearing blocks, Riftglass, crystal roots, sealed blocks, and decorative civic machinery. Keep functional redstone/recipe blocks visibly distinct from decor.
- **Sky/fog:** dimension-specific sky and fog that preserve silhouettes and visibility. Do not reduce visibility so far that combat telegraphs disappear.
- **Particles:** local, short-lived effects. Avoid continuous particles on every block or entity.
- **Boss presentation:** unique silhouette, intro cue, arena language, phase announcement, defeat effect, and readable loot drop. Avoid unskippable long cutscenes.

### Sound design

- Add distinct ambience per biome, a restrained dimension music pool, phase-transition cues, hazard warnings, structure interactions, and unique boss telegraphs.
- Every critical sound warning needs a visual equivalent and a subtitle. Sound is a supporting signal, never the only way to survive a mechanic.
- Ensure nearby players hear a successful interaction once, without duplicate local + server playback.
- Add config options for dimension music/ambient cues if consistent with existing config patterns; do not override the player's master volume.

### UI / guidebook / JEI

- Update the guidebook with entry requirements, safe-arrival guidance, biome/hazard knowledge, cycle states, mobs, boss preparation, crafting progression, relic effects, and all restrictions/stacking rules.
- Add JEI support for new recipes, upgrade templates, and machine pathways. Show catalyst-to-enchantment and catalyst-to-infusion routes separately.
- Tooltips must explain the effect, equipment slot, activation condition, cooldown, caps, incompatible effects, and whether an item is safe to use outside The Vestige.
- Provide debug-only visualization/commands for checking biome placement, structure starts, hazard regions, cycle state, and boss state. Do not expose developer debug controls as normal progression rewards.

## 11. Configuration and optional integrations

### Server/world configuration

Provide typed, commented, validated settings where applicable:

- Dimension-cycle total/phase durations and whether world cycles are enabled.
- Hazard exposure rate, decay, pulse damage, warning time, and enabled hazard types.
- Mob spawn multipliers, local caps, elite/event spawn rates, and boss health/damage scaling.
- Portal activation and return safety behavior; optional setting for development worlds to bypass the End advancement (default off).
- Relic cooldown multipliers and Curios behavior consistent with the 0.0.3 plan.
- Reliquary Loom upgrade costs and optional augment rules.
- Aetherforged gear balance values only where safe and supported by the code architecture; avoid exposing every raw constant as config without need.

### Client configuration

- Optional dimension ambience/music, particle density, visual distortion intensity, and minimal/full hazard UI.
- Ensure client options cannot affect server-side damage, loot, dimension progression, or recipe costs.

### Optional dependency handling

- Curios remains optional; test both present and absent. Use the compatible Forge 1.20.1 artifact and API, not a Forge/NeoForge or Minecraft-version mismatch.
- Any optional animation library must be guarded and cannot be required to render basic entities or load the dimension.
- Avoid hard links from common/server code to optional client-only classes. Test dedicated server startup explicitly.

## 12. Performance and reliability requirements

- Use per-dimension/per-level state for cycle and events; do not scan all entities/chunks every tick.
- Prefer event-driven hazard updates or low-frequency scheduled checks. Bound entity count, particles, path recalculation, and structure-based block-entity counts.
- Cache immutable biome/structure metadata. Do not cache mutable world/entity references across unloads.
- Avoid loading distant chunks just to find bosses, structures, ore, or active relics. Cycle effects only apply to already-loaded players/chunks.
- Use server-side spawn caps and despawn policies for event waves. No infinite spawn source, no runaway item drops, and no permanent block updates from environmental effects.
- Profile worldgen and boss encounters on a dedicated server with several players before release. Include a low-particle client setting for accessibility and weaker hardware.
- Check chunk save/reload, dimension transfer, server restart, death/respawn, disconnect/reconnect, and world upgrade paths for every persistent system.
- Do not change dimension/biome/structure IDs after release without a migration plan. Keep registries stable once published.

## 13. Phased implementation plan

The update is deliberately staged. Each phase should compile, launch, and be testable before the next adds more complexity.

### Phase 0 — Repository audit and design lock

- Inspect the 0.0.2 implementation and 0.0.3 plan: items, IDs, enchantments, infusions, machine recipes, tags, data components/NBT, configs, guidebook, JEI, tests, and version metadata.
- Create an inventory of all existing enchantment/infusion mappings, including the existing Iron Ingot and Nether Star mappings. Write tests or a machine-readable baseline to protect these IDs/outputs.
- Validate every row in Section 8.4 against the 0.0.3 planned source matrix. Identify existing recipe IDs before adding anything.
- Reserve and audit new IDs/namespaces for dimension, biomes, blocks, items, mobs, bosses, sounds, effects, recipes, structures, loot tables, and config keys.
- Confirm Forge 47.3.0+ API conventions in the repository and run the current build/tests before making changes. Save a clean baseline.

**Exit gate:** baseline build passes; mapping audit is documented; no duplicate IDs/names are planned.

### Phase 1 — Dimension skeleton and portal

- Register dimension type/level stem/worldgen resources and the Concordance Gate.
- Add safe arrival position, return travel, End advancement check, basic sky/fog/music, and dimension ID.
- Create one working biome (Crownfall Basin), one basic terrain style, one Waystone Monolith, and basic spawn rules.
- Add debug commands or safe test hooks for entering/exiting and inspecting dimension state.

**Exit gate:** new worlds and existing worlds can load the dimension; multiplayer travel works; no vanilla dimension is modified; restart and death recovery are safe.

### Phase 2 — Worldgen vertical slice

- Implement common terrain features, surface rules, biome placement, structure placement, and the full eight-biome roster incrementally.
- Add the first two structures and the Aetherite/Riftglass materials; verify generation borders, spacing, and safe exploration routes.
- Add biome-specific spawn tables and ambient presentation.

**Exit gate:** representative seeds have all planned biome types and structure types; no pervasive terrain holes, void spawns, structure collisions, or excessive worldgen lag.

### Phase 3 — Cycle, hazards, and preparation systems

- Implement Concordance Cycle saved state, phase transitions, multiplayer sync, HUD/guidebook feedback, and config handling.
- Implement Rift Saturation, Gravity Shear, Starfall Impact, Null Pockets, and Hushfield in isolated test areas first.

**Exit gate:** each hazard has warning, counterplay, configurable strength, and safe recovery; phase persists across restart without resetting progression or granting duplicate rewards.

### Phase 4 — Mob framework and first regional content

- Implement shared AI helpers, spawn caps, loot tables, sound/animation conventions, and persistence tests.
- Add the first 8–10 mobs and mini-boss framework; test pathfinding in all relevant terrain types.
- Add the introductory dungeon and the first boss, the Thorned Regent, end-to-end.

**Exit gate:** the first region can be explored, fought through, looted, and revisited without AI stalls or infinite spawning.

### Phase 5 — Full boss and structure roster

- Add remaining mob types and the 12+ major structures/encounter sites.
- Implement the Choir Unheard, Gravity Maw, Starforged Colossus, Warden of Seals, and Archivist-Prime as separate tested encounters.
- Add boss reward idempotency, arena recovery/reset behavior, rematches, and multiplayer scaling.

**Exit gate:** all six bosses can be reached, defeated, rewarded, and reset/revisited as designed; no progression softlocks on death or disconnect.

### Phase 6 — Materials, Aetherforged gear, and Relic Loom

- Add the full material pipeline, recipes, upgrade template, Aetherforged armor/tools, Reliquary Loom, six new relics, and their augmentation branches.
- Verify every new item has a clear drop/craft source and uses the intended progression tier.
- Test metadata preservation during smithing and relic augmentation.

**Exit gate:** early/mid/late progression is playable; gear values are benchmarked against Netherite; relic changes are reversible only where the design allows and never reset cooldowns.

### Phase 7 — New enchantments, infusions, and mapping validation

- Implement the six proposed 0.0.4 enchantments and four infusion effects after checking for duplicate names/effects.
- Register every row in the Section 8.5 source-item matrix as distinct enchantment and infusion recipe paths.
- Keep the Section 8.4 planned 0.0.3 matrix and all actual 0.0.2 mappings unchanged.
- Update Transmutation Table/Celestial Infuser recipes, JEI, tooltips, and guidebook from the canonical mapping definitions.

**Exit gate:** automated validation confirms every mapped source has both paths; no legacy recipe ID/output was replaced; infusion limit and stacking rules are consistent.

### Phase 8 — UX, visuals, sound, and compatibility

- Finish biome ambience, phase transitions, boss cues, particles, subtitles, dimension UI, guidebook, JEI, tooltips, and configs.
- Test optional Curios integration both present and absent; test dedicated server loading without client-only class references.
- Add low-particle/accessibility settings and error logging for invalid config/mapping data.

**Exit gate:** ordinary players can understand entry, hazards, cycles, crafting, bosses, and rewards without developer commands or external documentation.

### Phase 9 — Balance, performance, migration, and release

- Run a clean install, an upgrade-from-0.0.2/0.0.3 scenario where applicable, single-player test, dedicated-server test, and multiplayer test.
- Profile dimension exploration, structure generation, boss fights, phase transitions, and crowded event zones.
- Test all registry and saved-data stability checks, all acceptance tests, and recipes through JEI and actual crafting.
- Update mod version, changelog, metadata, and artifact names consistently to **0.0.4**. Do not bump to 0.0.4 before all source mappings and compatibility tests pass.

**Exit gate:** no critical world corruption, item loss, duplicate rewards, one-sided recipe mappings, client-only crashes, or progression softlocks.

## 15. Out-of-scope guardrails

This is a flagship update, but do not expand scope without documenting the cost and testing impact. Unless the existing codebase makes them trivial, defer these ideas to a later milestone:

- A second new dimension or a replacement for the Overworld, Nether, or End.
- Procedural quests, a full dialogue/civilization simulation, or large branching narrative campaign.
- Player-built world editing tools or a general-purpose scripting system.
- Infinite relic skill trees or random loot affixes without clear caps and deterministic crafting alternatives.
- A mandatory third-party mod dependency.
- Global changes to vanilla progression, existing 0.0.2 recipes, or planned 0.0.3 source mappings.

## 16. Definition of done

RelicWeave 0.0.4 is ready only when The Vestige is a distinct, explorable post-End dimension with eight functioning biomes, 12+ meaningful structures, 20+ distinct mob types, six completable bosses, working world cycles and hazards, new materials and Aetherforged progression, relic customization, six new enchantments, four new infusions, complete documentation, and passing compatibility/performance tests.

Most importantly, **the dimension must be playable from entry to final boss in a fresh world, all essential rewards must be obtainable, and every existing 0.0.2 and planned 0.0.3 mapping must remain intact.**
